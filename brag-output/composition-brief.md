## Creative Direction
- Tone preset: `cinematic`
- Creative direction: High-end enterprise product launch commercial. Think high-end motion studio producing an authoritative film for an important institution.
- Pacing: Deliberate, rhythmic, varying shot lengths, fast acceleration into settled holds, 2.7s final hold.
- Avoid: Generic floating cards, repetitive zooms, random particles, stock footage, fake cursors clicking meaningless buttons without real-world feedback, flat crossfades.
