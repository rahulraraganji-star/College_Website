# Brag Plan: Fr. Agnel College CMS (Creative Re-Direct)

## Phase 1 — Audit Findings & Creative Re-Direct
The previous video attempt suffered from standard SaaS promo tropes: centered static screenshots, repetitive fade-and-zoom transitions, disconnected floating pill cards, and simulated clicks on static images without demonstrating real product cause-and-effect.

### Detailed Audit:
1. **Generic Scenes**:
   - Scene 1 placed the public homepage inside a basic centered window with a generic bottom-left corner card.
   - Scenes 2, 3, and 4 reused an identical static centered frame (`1780x920`), swapping images via simple crossfades.
   - Scene 5 relied on a template-style circular radial blur and stacked text, lacking product integration.
2. **Repetitive Transitions**:
   - Every transition relied on the same `opacity: 0 / scale: 0.95` fade-out followed by an `opacity: 1 / scale: 1.0` fade-in.
   - Cuts between sub-features within the window were flat crossfades without camera continuity or spatial context.
3. **Missing Real Product Depth**:
   - The editor sequence simulated a click on "Save Changes" without showing what was edited or how it affected the live portal.
   - Workflows were shown as disconnected snapshots rather than a coherent administrative pipeline.
4. **Poorly Composed Screenshots**:
   - Downscaling full 1920x1080 screens into an inset 1780x920 frame caused micro-typography (sidebar navigation, timestamps, table rows) to become illegible.
   - The media library view had excessive empty placeholder area.
5. **Rebuild Strategy**:
   - Rebuilt layout architecture from the ground up: dynamic multi-layer stage, custom studio camera movements, authentic light and shadow modeling.
   - Replaced flat crossfades with intentional motion choreography: spatial glides, match cuts, and multi-surface workflow connections.
   - Created a dual-surface flagship finale showcasing the dark CMS alongside the light public portal.
